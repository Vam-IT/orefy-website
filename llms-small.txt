# Orefy (compact summary)

> Orefy is being developed to turn ClickOps AWS environments into Terraform, checked to zero diff. Not generally available yet.

- **In development**: read-only discovery, Terraform generation with import blocks, zero-diff verification (one account, one region).
- **Planned**: compliance scanning, multiple accounts and regions, OpenTofu, GitHub/GitLab handover with CI/CD.
- **Demo**: https://demo.orefy.cloud/ (simulated with example data, no sign-up).
- **Availability**: planned from Q1 2027, early access via info@orefy.cloud.
- **Pages**: https://orefy.cloud/ (English), https://orefy.cloud/de/ (German).
- **Operator**: VAMIT UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Bocholt, Germany.

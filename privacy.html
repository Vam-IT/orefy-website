IV. Who is affected by data processing?

When you visit our website as an interested party or visitor, personal data is processed strictly within statutory provisions and this policy. All visitors to our website are referred to as "Users".

V. What data do we process and for what purposes?

Our website is purely informational without user accounts, login, registration, or payment processing.

Hosting via GitHub Pages
Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (parent company: Microsoft Corporation). When you visit our website, your browser automatically transmits technical access data to GitHub's servers. This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, date and time of access, and the requested URL. This is technically necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the reliable, performant and secure operation of our website. The data is transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), so the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). We have no influence on how long GitHub stores this access data. For more information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Providing this data is required to access the website. Without it, the website cannot be displayed.

Local Delivery of Assets (No Third-Party CDNs or Fonts): All typography (Inter, JetBrains Mono), scripts, and assets are served directly through our repository on GitHub Pages. There is no dynamic loading from third parties such as Google Fonts or external CDNs.

Contact via E-Mail or Phone
If you contact us by email or phone, we process the data you provide in order to handle your inquiry. This includes your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your inquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries).

Our email mailbox is hosted by STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany. STRATO processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

No Automated Decision-Making
No automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR), takes place.

VI. Do we use Cookies?

Our website sets neither first-party nor third-party cookies. No data is stored in Local Storage or Session Storage on your device. No cross-site profiling or tracking takes place. Therefore, a cookie consent banner is not required under Section 25 (2) TDDDG.

VII. Storage Duration

GitHub's storage of access data is governed by its own policies (see Section V). We delete data from inquiries once they have been fully resolved, unless statutory retention obligations apply, such as commercial or tax-law retention periods for business correspondence.

VIII. What are your Rights?

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights towards us:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • Right to Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be forgotten") (Art. 17 GDPR);
  • Right to Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
Right to Object pursuant to Art. 21 GDPR

Where we process your data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process your data, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. An informal objection sent to info@orefy.cloud is sufficient.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy informs you about the processing of personal data when using our website. This privacy policy applies to our website. We do not operate any social media profiles or further online presences to which this policy would need to apply.

Personal data includes all information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (e.g. name, address, email address, IP address, user behavior). Regarding the terms used (such as "processing", "controller", or "data subject"), reference is made to the definitions in Art. 4 GDPR.

III. How do you reach our Data Protection Officer?

We are not legally obliged to appoint a Data Protection Officer. If you have questions regarding the processing of your data, you may contact us directly at any time (contact details see above).

IV. Who is affected by data processing?

When you visit our website as an interested party or visitor, personal data is processed strictly within statutory provisions and this policy. All visitors to our website are referred to as "Users".

V. What data do we process and for what purposes?

Our website is purely informational without user accounts, login, registration, or payment processing.

Completely Local Delivery (No Third-Country Transfers): All content, including typography (Inter, JetBrains Mono), scripts, and assets, is delivered entirely locally from our web server. When browsing our website, no data or IP addresses are transferred to external third parties (such as Google Fonts or external CDNs).

Server Log Files: When you access our website, technical connection data is automatically processed to display the website and ensure IT stability and security:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of server access
  • Name and URL of the requested file
  • Access status / HTTP response code
  • Transferred data volume
  • Referrer URL (previously visited website)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system

The processing of server log data is strictly necessary for delivering the website and maintaining security. Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in IT security and functionality).

Contact via E-Mail: If you contact us via email (e.g. at info@orefy.cloud), we process the data you voluntarily provide (email address, name, message content) to handle your inquiry. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR or Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR for contract-related requests.

VI. Do we use Cookies?

Our website sets neither first-party nor third-party cookies. No tracking or marketing data is stored in Local Storage or Session Storage. No cross-site profiling or tracking takes place. Therefore, a cookie consent banner is not required under Section 25 (2) TDDDG.

VII. Storage Duration

Server log files are processed short-term for IT security purposes and deleted routinely. Inquiries via email are deleted once the matter is conclusively resolved and no statutory retention requirements apply.

VIII. What are your Rights?

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights towards us:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • Right to Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be forgotten") (Art. 17 GDPR);
  • Right to Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR);
  • Right to Object on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).
